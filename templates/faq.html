FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Find answers to common questions about our services

Do you come to my home or workplace?

+

Yes! Panda Hub is a mobile detailing service. We come directly to your home, workplace, or any convenient location. Our mobile units are 100% self-contained with onboard water and power, so we don't need access to your utilities.

How long does detailing take?

+

Service duration varies by package: Essential takes 1.5-2 hours, Complete takes 3-4 hours, and Premium takes 5-6 hours. The exact time depends on your vehicle size and condition.

Do I need to provide water or electricity?

+

No, you don't need to provide anything. Our mobile units are fully self-contained with 100-gallon water tanks and quiet generators. We bring everything needed to detail your vehicle.

Can I book the same day?

+

Same-day appointments are often available based on our schedule. We recommend booking online to check real-time availability. Popular time slots may require advance booking.

Can you remove pet hair?

+

Yes, we specialize in pet hair removal. Our Complete and Premium packages include specialized electrostatic tools and hot-water extraction to remove even the most stubborn embedded pet hair from carpets and upholstery.

How often should I detail my car?

+

We recommend detailing your vehicle every 3-4 months for optimal protection and appearance. However, this depends on your driving conditions, climate, and personal preferences. Regular detailing helps maintain your vehicle's value and condition.

Do you detail SUVs and trucks?

+

Yes, we detail all vehicle types including sedans, SUVs, trucks, vans, and luxury vehicles. Our pricing adjusts based on vehicle size to ensure fair and transparent pricing.

Do you offer fleet/corporate services?

+

Yes, we can accommodate fleet and corporate services. Contact us directly to discuss custom packages for multiple vehicles, regular maintenance schedules, and corporate rates.

What areas do you serve?

+

We currently serve major Canadian metropolitan areas including the GTA, Metro Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton, Ottawa-Gatineau, Montreal, and Kitchener-Waterloo. Contact us to confirm service availability in your specific area.

What payment methods do you accept?

+

We accept credit cards, debit cards, and e-transfer. Payment is collected after service completion to ensure you're satisfied with the results.
