ABOUT PANDA HUB

Professional Detailing. We Come to You.

OUR STORY

Panda Hub Mobile Detailing was created around a simple idea: professional vehicle care should fit into the customer's schedule.

Traditional detailing can require customers to drive to a shop, leave their vehicle, wait for the service to be completed, and then return to collect it. Panda Hub's mobile model is designed to remove that inconvenience by bringing detailing directly to the customer's preferred location.

Our focus is simple: Convenience. Professional Care. A Better Customer Experience.

We provide structured detailing packages so customers can easily choose the level of service that fits their vehicle and needs. From regular vehicle maintenance to deeper interior and exterior detailing, Panda Hub is designed around clear service options and an easy digital booking experience.

OUR FUTURE

Panda Hub is being developed as a customer-first mobile detailing concept with the potential to expand into recurring maintenance, fleet and corporate services as demand grows.

Our autonomous mobile detailing units are equipped with 100-gallon spot-free deionized water tanks, quiet generators, 220°F high-temp dry vapor steam boilers, and heated extractors. We serve major Canadian metropolitan hubs including the GTA, Vancouver, Calgary, Montreal, and Ottawa.
